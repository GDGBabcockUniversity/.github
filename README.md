## Welcome to GDG Babcock

We are **Google Developer Groups On Campus — Babcock University**, a student-led tech community bringing together developers, designers and technology enthusiasts.

We learn by building, grow through collaboration and turn ideas into real-world solutions.

Our community is a space for students to explore technology, contribute to open source and connect with people who share their passion for innovation.

## What We Do

- **Build** — Create open-source projects and real-world solutions.
- **Learn** — Explore AI, software development, cloud computing and emerging technologies.
- **Connect** — Collaborate with developers, share knowledge and grow together.
- **Innovate** — Participate in hackathons, workshops and technical challenges.
